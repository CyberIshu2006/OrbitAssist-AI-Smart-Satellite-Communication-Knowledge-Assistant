01 — Project Introduction
Problem

Knowledge Gap in Satellite Tech

Satellite communication is a complex, highly specialized domain. Engineers, analysts, and students struggle to quickly access accurate technical knowledge about frequency bands, orbit types, link budgets, and communication systems — often scattered across dense manuals.

AI Solution

Intelligent Knowledge Assistant

OrbitAssist AI is a lightweight NLP-powered assistant that understands natural language queries and instantly retrieves structured, expert-level satellite communication knowledge — making technical expertise accessible to everyone on the team.

Key Capabilities

What OrbitAssist Can Do

  • Natural language query understanding
  • Frequency band encyclopedia (L to Ka)
  • Orbit type comparison (LEO, MEO, GEO)
  • Communication concepts explained
  • Real-world application knowledge
  • Structured, concise AI responses
